Support

Contact us

Found a bug, have a question or want to suggest something? Open an issue on GitHub. Tell us your device, its system version, and what happened. We usually reply within a few business days.

Getting started

Switch on the Tutorial on the title screen and press Start Tutorial. The walkthrough builds every district, uses every unit and takes you through an invasion, with a callout on each unit explaining what it does. Turn on Auto-play to let it run hands-free, or press End Tutorial at any time. Once it finishes, the tutorial switches itself off; turn it back on from the title screen whenever you like.

Controls

  • Touch: tap to select, tap the map to move or attack, pinch to zoom, drag to pan.
  • Mouse and keyboard (Mac and iPad): click to select, click the map to move or attack, scroll to zoom. Each district has a hotkey, shown on its button.
  • Game controllers are supported too.

How to win

Each cycle your island earns credits and your citizens award approval points. Keep FOOD and HOUSING ahead of your population, keep the people content, and either out-govern your rival over your term or capture their citadel.

Requirements

iOS 17 or later (iPhone), iPadOS 17 or later (iPad), macOS 14 or later (Mac).
