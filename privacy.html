Privacy Policy

Dystopia for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Effective November 22, 2026

Future Pixels Studio LLC (“we”, “us”) makes Dystopia. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Summary

Dystopia does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What we don’t collect

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking by us or by third parties.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Data on your device

The game keeps a small amount of settings on your device, such as whether the tutorial is switched on and your sound preferences. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Network use

Dystopia plays entirely offline and does not connect to our servers or anyone else’s.

Purchases

You buy Dystopia through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment under its own privacy policy, and we receive no payment card or personal details.

Children

Dystopia is intended for players aged 14 and up. Because we collect no personal data, we collect none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/alexsto/dystopia/issues.
